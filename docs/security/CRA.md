# The EU Cyber Resilience Act: a decision record

Prepared by the AurOS agents without a lawyer (D15). `docs/legal/COMPLIANCE.md` SL-1, SL-2, BIZ-10.
Regulation (EU) 2024/2847: the manufacturer reporting duty applies from 11 Sep 2026, most other
duties from 11 Dec 2027. Citations were read from secondary sources (EUR-Lex was not readable
during the review); re-read before relying on them.

**Facts (v0.1).** Free, MIT, an individual maintainer, no company, no price anywhere, no paid-only
release, update or fix, English only, offered for the United States for now, no EU marketing.
Free and open-source software supplied **outside a commercial activity** is out of scope; an
individual maintainer is not a "steward". Commission guidance (July 2026, non-binding): donations,
sponsorships, public funding and paid consulting, training or support do not by themselves make a
project commercial; selling the software, paid editions, monetising services delivered through the
software, or demanding personal data beyond security needs do.

**Rule.** *No paid-only releases, security fixes or updates. A paid Wishes quota reached from the
free shell is the CRA trigger: shipping it first needs a manufacturer-status decision (or sale only
in the US, UK, Canada and Australia, with the merchant of record's territory excluding the EU).
Paid human help and donations are not the trigger.*

**What already serves the story:** an SBOM per release (WP-68), a declared support period (Ubuntu 24.04
security until April 2029, conditional on the project continuing: `docs/security/UPDATES.md`), the
reporting playbook (`docs/ops/INCIDENTS.md`), `SECURITY.md` and a contact point (needs the mailbox:
`CONTACT_EMAIL`).

**Before any sale of AurOS itself or pre-installed computers:** a notified-body quote (tens of
thousands of euros, unverified) or exclude the EU from the sale. Operating systems are Annex III
class I (third-party assessment unless harmonized standards are applied; unverified).
