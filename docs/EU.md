# The EU: when to open it

Prepared by the AurOS agents without a lawyer (D15). AurOS is in English and offered for the United
States for now (`docs/legal/TERMS.md` 1). Ireland and Malta are English-speaking EU states, so
"English-speaking countries" does not exclude the EU: the wave plan says **United States only** for
waves 1-2 (`docs/legal/COMPLIANCE.md` LIA-3; the owner's decision, checklist 16).

**Reopen triggers** (any one): a first paid Wishes quota; a first image or hardware sale; a donation
required for updates; Wishes collecting personal data beyond need; EU marketing or an EU-language
page. Not a trigger: a voluntary donation; an EU resident downloading from the public page.

**Before the EU:** the CRA decision (`docs/security/CRA.md`), the Product Liability Directive and
the EU Accessibility Act work (`docs/ACCESSIBILITY-CONFORMANCE.md`, WP-66/67), an authorised
representative if needed, the UK/EU privacy items (`docs/legal/COMPLIANCE.md` PRI-6), a scoping
consult with an EU lawyer (estimate $1,000-3,000, unverified). The fence meanwhile: a country check
on `/v1/register` and on paid offers, "offered in the US" on the download page and in the Terms.
