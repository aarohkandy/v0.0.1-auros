## AurOS is free, and comes as it is

AurOS is free software, made by a small team and given away without charge. It comes as it is, without a guarantee. We test it before each version goes on this website, but we can't test every computer, and we can't promise that it will work on yours or that nothing will go wrong.

## Copy your important files first

Installing AurOS changes how your computer's drive is divided. It is made to keep Windows and your files and to let you go back, but any change to a drive carries some risk. Before you start, copy anything you can't replace, such as photos and papers, to a memory stick or another drive.

## What we have tested, and what we haven't

Before a version of AurOS goes on this website, the whole install is run on a virtual computer that has Windows 11 on it. We also test putting Windows back, and cutting the power at each step, on pretend disks. AurOS has not yet been tried on a real, physical computer, and computers differ a great deal. That is one more reason to copy your photos first.
