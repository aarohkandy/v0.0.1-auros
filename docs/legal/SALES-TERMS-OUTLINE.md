# Terms of sale: the outline (for the day something is sold)

Prepared by the AurOS agents without a lawyer (D15). Not in force; nothing is sold. `COMPLIANCE.md`
LIA-8, PRI-8, BIZ-7/8/9 and `MONETISATION-GATE.md` say when this is needed (before any price,
donation link, paid help or paid Wishes quota exists). Fill it into a real document, with a
merchant of record's standard consumer terms, before the first price is shown.

1. **Price first.** The price and tax are on screen before any card is entered; hosted checkout off
   GitHub Pages; no card data in AurOS systems or the Worker.
2. **No surprise renewals.** One-off or prepaid purchases in the first version; no auto-renewal until
   a flow with the price, the renewal date and a one-click cancel exists.
3. **Refunds.** 30 days, no questions; an unused prepaid period is refunded on request; a wish
   that did not reach her Yes screen is never charged.
4. **Receipts** by email; kept as long as the tax adviser says (not a fixed 7 years).
5. **Help is by email or in-app chat.** No phone line, no outbound calls or emails selling anything.
   **Remote help** (if it is ever sold): only from a button she presses inside AurOS, scheduled, with
   a consent screen, no recording by default, a visible indicator and a red Stop bar; the public
   promise stays "Nobody from AurOS will ever phone you, email or text you first, ask you to call a
   number, or connect to your computer" (BIZ-7). Many payment providers refuse human technical
   support: confirm in writing before relying on one.
6. **What is sold is not a safety promise.** More wishes a day, not accuracy; the AI can misunderstand.
7. **The privacy page changes first** (a paid service ties a payer to a use): the processor is an
   independent controller; payment events are logged by the merchant's order id only.
8. **Where.** The US, UK, Canada and Australia only until the EU duties are decided; the merchant's
   sales territory excludes the EU/EEA (SL-1/2, LIA-4).
9. **Tax.** Digital services are taxed in the buyer's place (UK VAT for a non-UK seller from the first
   sale; US economic nexus about $100,000 or 200 transactions per state, and 200 transactions
   arrive first at $5 credits). A merchant of record handles it; check which products it covers (BIZ-8).
10. **Terms of use.** `TERMS.md` section 1 stays "AurOS itself is free. Some extras may cost money
    later, and we will say so clearly before you pay for anything."; "no subscription" on the site
    is removed in the commit that creates the first price.
