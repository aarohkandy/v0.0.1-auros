## What is built (tests green on homebase)

**The panel** (`wish.c`, 12 pages): consent; ask (large box with caret, microphone
"Say it", six examples the first time and three after, "Pick from a list"); listening
(tap to start and stop, at most 30 s, no wake word); hearing; waiting (3-segment
stepping bar, gives up at 20 s, "Stop asking"); the confirmation (You said, which she
can press to change; the device's own sentence with `*bold*`; Yes and No the same
size and no default focus; Show me; Read it to me; the destination in large type for an
outward action); cannot; the help answer; done (Undo my last wish for 10 minutes);
errors (offline, busy, resting, limit, refused, slow, broken, unsure, nomic: one
plain line each); My wishes (On/Off switch with the word, Remove with a question,
Turn off all wishes, Put everything back how it was); **the gallery** (20 wishes she
presses with no internet, up to two blanks each: picture, word, time, message;
`wishgallery.c`; sent as op `check`; offered from the ask page and from every
error/cannot page); **What my wishes did today** (with Stop all wishes).
Layout is one pure function per surface; when her largest text does not fit the
smallest screen the panel drops its heading, then its small labels, then steps its
words down a tenth at a time, so no button is ever off screen (tested at 3 sizes ×
4 text sizes × all pages: every target ≥ 48 px, on screen, not overlapping).

**Overlays** (`wishover.c`): the fill (emoji from the image's Noto Color Emoji via
CBLC/CBDT + a bounded inflate + PNG decoder, or words; ≤ 10 s; the first key or press
is used up, with its release); cards (message, remind with "This was for 9:00",
private reminders, Remind me in 10 minutes, "Keep this wish?", **Undo my last
wish** for 10 minutes after a `now` wish); a local "Keep this?" after a look or
text-size change that puts it back after 15 s. Each says `Your wish "X"`, has a 48 px
"Stop this wish", never takes the keyboard, is at most half the screen, one per wish.

**Socket client** to docs/AI.md §3.7: hello, consent, wish, cancel, confirm, discard,
list, enable, remove, test, all_off/all_on, undo, restore, keep, snooze, today, check,
did; events triggers, changed, fill, message, remind, notice, keep_it, shell. Also
`input` (≤ once a second) and `hold` (`password` while the Wi-Fi panel is open).
`shell` events carried out here: set_text_size, set_look (four looks, checked in one
place), open_app, open_website (https, no quotes). Not carried out, answered
`ok:false`, nothing faked: set_wallpaper, night_light, pointer_size,
do_not_disturb (the shell has no control for them yet).

**Hooks:** "Make a wish" entry (ICON_WISH, APP_ROLE_WISH): a tile/row just before
Get more programs on rail, tiles, workbench and the taskbar's All my programs list;
a labelled button on the dock beside Get more programs; a button on the taskbar right
after All my programs; a Settings row "My wishes". `wishes="off"` in the policy
(and a kiosk) removes all of it. `wish_open` and `wishfill_open` are in
SHELL_PANEL_OPEN. `c->want_wish` (WISH_CMD_TODAY / WISH_CMD_STOP_ALL) is for buttons
outside Wishes — see Needs.

**aurvoice** (`src/aurwish/voice.c`): `aurvoice listen [--max N]` records into memory
only (an anonymous memory file is the recogniser's only input; no core dump; wiped
before exit), stops on SIGUSR1, cancels on SIGTERM, prints one line
`heard <words>` | `unsure` | `nomic` | `broken`; silent/short recordings and the
phrases a speech model invents from nothing are `unsure`. `aurvoice say <text>` speaks
with piper if installed, espeak-ng if not. The model, voices and measurements are in
`docs/VOICE.md`.

Scratch scripts live in the lead's scratchpad (`w2/`: `h` ssh, `sync`, `dk` run in the

## Traps

- **The layout does not know the font.** Geometry is a pure function (so the painter, the
  hit test and `tools/targets.c` agree), so it estimates a glyph as 0.56 em and a line as
  1.2 to 1.5 em, while Atkinson Hyperlegible Next is 0.44 to 0.50 em wide and 1.3 em from
  ascent to descent. Anything painted by a rule other than the one it was laid out by gets
  cut off. `tools/wishpainttest.sh` paints everything in the real face and fails on any cut
  word or elided button; it found headings that vanished at 150 % text and above (the heading
  box was 1.2 em and `draw_wrapped` paints nothing into a box shorter than the font), a
  confirmation sentence that stopped mid-way, and a "Remo..." button. Run it after touching
  any layout. Sentences that must be whole (the device's, the one offered after "I can't do
  that, but...") are drawn by `draw_fitted`, which steps the size down to 18 px before it
  would cut anything.
- **Cards and Messages share a corner.** Stream C's message card hangs in the corner with no
  button under it (`notify_view_now`: top right, or bottom right where the clock is down in the
  bar and in One thing at a time). Wish cards are bottom right, so where C's is at the bottom
  they go bottom left (`cards_left()` in wishover.c repeats that rule; change one, change the
  other; `wishuitest` checks the three cases).

- A reply for a request she cancelled is ignored (the id no longer matches).
- `snprintf(a, n, "%s", a)` empties `a` (it did, after consent: found by the test).
- `shell.h` has `wishfill_open` (not `wish_fill`): tools/modaltest.c requires every
  `*_open` field in SHELL_PANEL_OPEN and in its table; and it hunts for guards written
  as a list of `c.<panel>_open` in main.c (the screen signature is a function for this
  reason).
- `git rebase --abort` in a push loop throws away a half-resolved rebase. The push
  script now aborts only a rebase it started.
- The mutation list in `tools/wishuitest.sh` must match the source text exactly
  (a mutant that does not apply is reported, not skipped).
- Homebase's disk filled to 100 % once; keep temporaries in `/dev/shm`.
