# Scoring sheet

Print one per person, in landscape. Fill in pen. The clock is **Clock A**
(`SCRIPT.md`, section 1): `mm:ss` from the moment the link is put in front of
her. "Hes." is a tally of pauses over 10 seconds. "Errors" is a tally of wrong
presses, misses, double presses and presses that undid something. The aid
column is the highest level given on that step (`SCRIPT.md`, 4.3).

told about. One sentence each, and the sheet.
