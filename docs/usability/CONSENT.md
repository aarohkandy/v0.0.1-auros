# Trying out AurOS: what we will do, and your say in it

*Print this in large type (18 point or more), one page, one side. Read it
aloud with the person. Keep a copy for her. Prepared by the AurOS team
without a lawyer's review (decision D15); if the owner has it reviewed,
record that in `docs/legal/README.md`.*

---

**What this is.** AurOS is a new free program that gives a Windows computer a
different, simpler way of working. We want to find the places where it is
confusing. **We are testing the program. We are not testing you.** Nothing
you do can be wrong.

**What we will ask you to do.** To put AurOS on a computer, to look for a
photo, to make a video call, to make the words bigger, and to put Windows
back. It takes up to two and a half hours, with a break. You can stop at any
moment, for any reason, and you do not have to say why. Nothing bad happens
if you stop. You will still get the thank-you.

**What could go wrong, plainly.** AurOS is new. Putting it on a computer
changes the computer. We have built it so that your Windows and your files
stay, and so that you can go back. We still cannot promise that nothing will
ever go wrong. So, before we start:

- we will offer to copy your photos and documents onto a drive that we give
  you, so that they are safe whatever happens;
- we will check, at the end, that every file is where it was;
- if anything is lost, we will tell you at once and put it back from the copy.

You can say no to any part. If you do not want AurOS on your own computer,
we can use ours.

**What we write down.** We film the *screen and your hands on the mouse and keyboard*
(not your face) so that we do not have to guess what happened. With your
permission we also record your *voice*. We write down how long things take and what you say. We do not
need your passwords. If the computer asks for a password or a PIN, we will
look away and stop the recording while you type it.

**Who sees it.** Only the AurOS team. Nothing we publish has your name on it
or can be traced to you. We may quote something you said, without your name.
We keep the recordings for [six months] and then delete them. You can ask us
to delete yours at any time: write to [contact, to be filled in by the owner].

**Someone you trust** may sit in the room. Please ask them to let you try
by yourself and not to help while you do. We need to see what happens when
nobody is there.

**You do not have to sign this to leave.** If you would rather not take part,
you may go now with our thanks.
