It is stuck, frozen or dark

Most things are put right in a moment. Find what you see below.

One program has stopped answering

Press Close this at the bottom of the screen. Count to three. Still there? Press Close this again, and once more if it is still there. AurOS then stops it. Unsaved work in it may be lost.

The screen went dark

It goes dark by itself after about ten minutes of nobody touching the computer. Press any key, or move the mouse. The first press only wakes the screen.

A laptop with a flat battery also goes dark. Plug in the charger, wait a few minutes, then press the power button once.

Nothing on the screen answers

  1. Wait a minute. A busy computer can seem stuck.
  2. If the bar at the bottom answers, press Turn off, then Start it again.
  3. If not, hold the computer’s power button down for about ten seconds, until the screen goes dark. Wait a moment, then press it once. AurOS starts again. What you saved is kept.

Not while the screen is black with lines of small words. Then AurOS is being put on this computer. Wait, even if it takes long.

AurOS doesn’t start

Switch the computer off, wait a moment, and switch it on again. To use Windows meanwhile, choose Windows in the short list of names that shows for a few seconds when the computer starts. Then ask someone who knows computers to help.

Still stuck? Who do I ask? To finish, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
