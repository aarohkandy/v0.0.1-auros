If something looks wrong

Most things are put right in a moment. Find what you see below.

A program has stopped answering

Press Close this at the bottom of the screen. If the program is still there after a moment, press Close this again. AurOS then stops it for you. Anything you hadn’t saved in it may be lost.

The words are too small, or too big

Press Bigger text or Smaller text at the bottom right of the screen, as many times as you like. Nothing else changes.

In Internet, and on these pages, hold down Ctrl and press + to make the words bigger. Ctrl and − makes them smaller.

There is no sound

Press Settings at the bottom of the screen, then How loud the sound is, and make it louder. If headphones are plugged in, the sound goes to them instead.

A form lost the last thing you typed

When you fill in a PDF form on the screen, in Documents, press the Tab key once after you type in the last box. Then save. Without it, the last box can come back empty.

The internet isn’t working

See Getting online.

A page says your computer has a problem

If a page says your computer has a virus or is locked, or tells you to call a number, it is a trick. A web page cannot tell whether your computer has a virus. Don’t call. Press Help, then Get me out of this page. AurOS will never call you says more.

Nothing on the screen answers

  1. Press Turn off, then Start it again.
  2. If even the bar at the bottom doesn’t answer, hold the computer’s power button down for about ten seconds, until the screen goes dark.
  3. Wait a moment, then press the power button once. AurOS starts again. Anything you hadn’t saved may be lost. The things you saved are kept.

AurOS doesn’t start

Switch this computer off. Wait a moment, then switch it on again. If AurOS still doesn’t start, ask someone who knows computers to help.

Still not right?

Ask someone you trust who knows computers to help, and show them this page. The people who make AurOS have no phone line, and they will never call you.

When you have finished reading, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
