If something looks wrong

Don’t worry, and don’t rush. Most things are put right in a moment. Find what you see below.

A program has stopped answering

Press Close this at the bottom of the screen. If it is still there after a moment, press Close this again: AurOS then stops it for you. Anything you hadn’t saved in it may be lost.

The words are too small, or too big

Press Bigger or Smaller at the bottom right of the screen, as many times as you like. Nothing else changes.

In Firefox, and on these pages: hold down Ctrl and press + to make the words bigger, or Ctrl and − to make them smaller.

There is no sound

Press Settings at the bottom of the screen, then How loud the sound is, and make it louder. If headphones are plugged in, the sound goes to them instead.

The internet isn’t working

See Getting online.

A page says your computer has a problem

If a page says your computer has a virus or is locked, or tells you to call a number: it is a trick, and your computer is fine. Don’t call. Press Close this. AurOS will never call you says more.

Nothing on the screen answers

  1. Press Turn off, then Start it again.
  2. If even the bar at the bottom doesn’t answer, hold the computer’s power button down for about ten seconds, until the screen goes dark.
  3. Wait a moment, then press the power button once. AurOS starts again. Anything you hadn’t saved may be lost, but the things you saved are kept.

AurOS doesn’t start

When the computer switches on, a short list appears. Choose Windows in it to use Windows instead. Going back to Windows shows how.

Still not right?

Ask someone you trust to help: a friend, a neighbour or someone in your family. Show them this page.

The people who make AurOS answer questions on the internet, on the AurOS project page on GitHub, under “Issues”. They have no phone line, and they will never call you. Never post passwords or private details there: everyone can read it.

When you have finished reading, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
