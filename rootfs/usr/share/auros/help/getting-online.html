Getting online

This computer gets online through your Wi-Fi, or through a cable from your internet box.

Join your Wi-Fi

  1. At the bottom of the screen, press Wi-Fi.
  2. Wait a moment. A list of names appears.
  3. Press the name of your Wi-Fi. It is usually on a sticker on the bottom or back of your internet box, next to “Network” or “Wi-Fi name”.
  4. Type the password. It is usually on the same sticker, next to “Key” or “Password”. Capital letters matter, so copy it exactly. Then press Connect.
  5. When it says you are connected, you are online. This computer remembers, and joins by itself next time.

If it doesn’t work

  • It says the password did not work. Type it again, slowly. A zero and the letter O look alike. So do the number 1, a small L and a capital I.
  • Your Wi-Fi isn’t in the list. Move closer to the internet box and check that its lights are on. Then press Wi-Fi again.
  • It says this computer has no Wi-Fi. Use a cable instead.

Use a cable

Plug a network cable from your internet box into the matching socket on this computer. It gets online by itself within a minute. There is nothing to type.

Is it working?

Open Internet, the program for websites, from your home screen. If a page appears, you are online.

When you have finished reading, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
