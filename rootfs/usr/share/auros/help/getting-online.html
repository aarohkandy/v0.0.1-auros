Getting on the internet

Use your Wi-Fi, or a cable from your internet box.

Join your Wi-Fi

Do this the first time, and again in a new home, or with a new internet box.

  1. At the bottom of the screen, press Wi-Fi.
  2. Wait a moment. A list of names appears. Press the name of your Wi-Fi. It is on a sticker on your internet box, next to “Network” or “Wi-Fi name”.
  3. Type the password from the same sticker, next to “Key” or “Password”. Capital letters matter. Then press Connect.
  4. When it says you are connected, open Internet to check. Next time, this computer joins by itself.

Or use a cable. Plug a network cable from your internet box into this computer. It joins by itself within a minute. There is nothing to type.

If it doesn’t work

  • The password did not work. Type it again, slowly. A zero and the letter O look alike. So do the number 1 and a small L.
  • Your Wi-Fi isn’t in the list. Move closer to the internet box, then press Wi-Fi again.
  • You are joined, but no page opens. (If you can’t see Internet, press All my programs.) Switch your internet box off, count to ten, and switch it on. Wait two minutes.

Still stuck? Who do I ask? To finish, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
