Going back to Windows

Windows is still on this computer, with your files from before.

Use Windows once

Nothing is removed. AurOS starts again after that.

  1. Press Settings at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Press Put Windows back.
  3. Press Start Windows this once. This computer restarts into Windows.

If you change your mind, press Keep AurOS. Nothing changes.

Remove AurOS

This removes AurOS and gives Windows all of its space back. Your documents, pictures, music and videos are copied into Windows first. Your Desktop and Downloads are copied too. They go into a folder called From AurOS, inside your own folder in Windows. The programs you added to AurOS, and the way you set it up, do not come with them.
