Getting a new program

New programs come from Get more programs. They are free, and you don’t need a password.

Find Get more programs

Its picture is a bag with a plus sign. Where it is depends on your look:

  • Like Windows: press All my programs. It is the last one in the list.
  • Like a Mac: it is the button after your favourite programs.
  • Big and simple: it is the last line on your home page.
  • One thing at a time: it is the last big button on the first page.

Get a program

  1. Press Get more programs. The first time, it can take a minute or two to fill up. You need to be on the internet: see Getting on the internet.
  2. Press the magnifying glass at the top, and type the name of the program. For example, VLC plays videos.
  3. Press the program, then press Install. A big program takes a few minutes.
  4. When it is ready, press Open. From now on it is with your other programs too.

A program made for Windows does not work here. If you open such a file, AurOS says so and opens Get more programs. Look there for a program that does the same job.

Anyone who asks you to get a program so they can “see your screen” is tricking you. See Someone phoned about my computer.
