Turning it off

Turn this computer off with its own button on the screen. Don’t pull out the plug.

  1. At the bottom right of the screen, press Turn off.
  2. The screen asks “What should this computer do?” Press Turn it off.
  3. Wait. The screen goes dark and the computer switches itself off.

The other buttons on that screen

Start it again
The computer goes off and comes straight back on.
Let it sleep
The screen goes dark. Press a key to wake it. This button is there only if this computer can sleep.
Never mind
You go back to what you were doing.

The computer’s own power button

Press it once, and the computer turns itself off properly, the same as Turn it off. On a laptop, closing the lid puts it to sleep. Open the lid and press a key to wake it.

If the screen stays on and nothing answers, see If it is stuck, frozen or dark.

Still stuck? Who do I ask? To finish, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
