Remove AurOS and put Windows back

This removes AurOS and gives Windows all of its space back.

Your documents, pictures, music and videos are copied into Windows first. Your Desktop and Downloads are copied too. They go into a folder called From AurOS, inside your own folder in Windows.

The programs you added to AurOS, and the way you set it up, do not come with them. Neither do files kept anywhere else.

  1. Press Settings at the bottom of the screen, then Put Windows back.
  2. Press Remove AurOS and put Windows back. Its words change to Press again to restart and remove AurOS. Read them, then press it again.
  3. AurOS copies your files first. This can take a while. If it cannot copy them all, it says so and removes nothing. Then press Save my files to a USB stick first, put in a memory stick, and try again.
  4. This computer restarts and removes AurOS. Leave it switched on and plugged in until Windows has started. If a window says it is putting your files in Windows, leave the computer on until it is done.

Photos you could not replace are safest with one more copy on a memory stick.

After you pressed No, go back to Windows, AurOS is still on this computer, but Windows starts. To use AurOS again, or to remove it, ask someone who knows computers.
