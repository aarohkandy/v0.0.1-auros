Bigger words, louder sound

Nothing here can break anything. You can always change it back.

Make the words bigger

  1. At the bottom right of the screen, press Bigger text. Press it again, as many times as you like.
  2. A program that was already open may keep its small words. Press Close this, then open it again.

Too big? Press Smaller text. The bar is always there, even when everything else is too big. In Internet, and on these pages, you can also hold down Ctrl and press + or −.

Make the sound louder

Press Settings, then How loud the sound is, and make it louder. If it was at nothing, the sound comes back. Keys with a picture of a speaker work too.

  • If headphones are plugged in, the sound goes to them. For wireless ones, press Headphones and mice in Settings.
  • No How loud the sound is in Settings? Then this computer cannot change its sound. Ask someone you trust.

Still stuck? Who do I ask? To finish, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
