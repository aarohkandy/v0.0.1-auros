I forgot my PIN

Some computers ask for a PIN when AurOS starts. If you forgot yours, the paper you kept gets you in.

AurOS asks for a PIN

The screen says “Type your PIN to start AurOS”. This help can’t open before AurOS has started, so that screen has its own buttons. These are the steps for it. Take the paper you kept when you chose your PIN. It has a rescue code on it: six groups of four numbers.

  1. Press I forgot my PIN.
  2. Type the numbers from your paper. Press OK.
  3. Choose your PIN again. Pick 4 to 6 numbers you will remember. It can be the same PIN as before. Type it twice. Then AurOS starts.

If it says the code didn’t work, check the numbers against your paper, slowly. Remembered your PIN after all? Press Back to my PIN.

After too many wrong tries, the computer makes you wait about ten minutes. Press Use my rescue code to go on at once.

No PIN and no paper

Then AurOS cannot be opened. The people who make AurOS have no copy of either. That keeps your things safe if the computer is stolen. Windows is not locked by this PIN: switch the computer off and on, and choose Windows in the short list of names that shows for a few seconds. Then ask someone you trust.

A PIN from Windows

If the PIN screen is Windows’, AurOS doesn’t know that PIN. Windows has its own way to start again, under the box where you type.

Still stuck? Who do I ask? To finish, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
