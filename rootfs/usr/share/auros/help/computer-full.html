The computer is getting full

There is not much room left for new things. Nothing is broken, and nothing of yours has been lost.

Make some room

  1. Press Your files. Pictures and videos take the most room. Look in Downloads first: things you downloaded once are often not needed again.
  2. Press a file you do not need, then press the Delete key. It goes into the Trash, where it still takes room.
  3. Press Trash, then Empty Trash, to get the room back.

Only remove what you are sure about. Your own photos and letters stay where they are unless you press them yourself. If you are not sure, leave it and ask someone you trust.
