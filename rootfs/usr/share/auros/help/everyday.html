Music, letters and email

If you can’t see a program, press All my programs.

Play music or watch a video

For a song or a film on this computer, press Your files, find it, and open it. It plays in Music and video.

For music or films on a website, open Internet and go to that website. No sound? See Bigger words, louder sound.

Write a letter

Press Letters. A blank page opens. Type your letter. When you close it, it asks whether to keep it. Press Save, and give your letter a name.

Letters also opens Word files. To print, see Printing.

Email

AurOS has no separate email program. Use your email on its website, in Internet, as you did on Windows. You may need to sign in again, with the same address and password as before.

A PDF form lost the last thing you typed

When you fill in a PDF form on the screen, in Documents, press the Tab key once after you type in the last box. Then save. Without it, the last box can come back empty.

Still stuck? Who do I ask? To finish, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
