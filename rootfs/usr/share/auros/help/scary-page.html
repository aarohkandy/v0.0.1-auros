A page says my computer is infected

A page says your computer has a virus, is locked, or is in danger. It may shout, and give a number to call.

It is a trick. A web page cannot tell whether your computer has a virus. Don’t take or open anything it offers you.

Get away from the page

  1. Don’t call the number, and don’t press anything on the page.
  2. Press Help at the bottom left of the screen. That bar is always there, whatever the page says.
  3. Press Get me out of this page. The sound goes off.
  4. Press Close this page. The page is gone.

If you can’t reach Help, press the Esc key three times, quickly. The same screen opens.

If the noise won’t stop, or the page comes back

Press Turn off at the bottom right, then Turn it off. Switch the computer on again a little later. If the page is back when you open Internet, do the same again, and ask someone you trust to look.

If you already called, or typed something in, see If you already let someone in.

Still stuck? Who do I ask? To finish, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
