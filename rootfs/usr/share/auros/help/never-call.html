AurOS will never call you

Nobody from AurOS will ever phone you, email you or show you a message asking you to call a number.

AurOS will never ask you:

  • to call a phone number;
  • to let someone connect to your computer, or to get a program so they can “see your screen”;
  • for money, card numbers, bank details, or gift cards;
  • for your passwords, or for a code sent to your phone.

AurOS is free. There is nothing to pay, ever. Anyone who asks for any of these things is not from AurOS, Microsoft, or your bank, whatever they say.

A page that says your computer has a problem

A website may fill the screen with a warning, perhaps with a loud noise, saying your computer has a virus or is locked, and giving a number to call. It is fake. Your computer is fine.

  1. Don’t call the number, and don’t press anything on the page.
  2. Press Close this at the bottom of the screen. That bar always works, whatever the page says.
  3. If the page or the noise won’t go: press Turn off at the bottom right, then Turn it off. Switch on again later.
  4. If the page comes back when you open Firefox, press Close this again, and ask someone you trust to look.

A phone call about your computer

People call pretending to be from Microsoft, AurOS, your internet company, the police or your bank, and say there is a problem with your computer or your account. Just hang up. A real company won’t mind. If you are worried, call the company back yourself, on a number you already have, such as the one on your bank card or your bill.

If you already let someone in

It happens to careful people every day, and it isn’t your fault. Act quickly:

  1. Press Turn off, then Turn it off. That cuts them off.
  2. Call your bank on the number on the back of your card. Tell them what happened.
  3. From another phone or computer, change the passwords for your email and your bank.
  4. Ask someone you trust to help you check this computer before you use it for banking again. You can also report it to the police.

When you have finished reading, press Close this at the bottom of the screen.
