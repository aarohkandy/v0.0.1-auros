Someone phoned about your computer

Hang up. Nobody from AurOS will ever phone you, or ask you to call a number.

What to do

People phone pretending to be from Microsoft, AurOS, your internet company, the police or your bank. They say your computer has a virus. It is a trick. A real company won’t mind if you hang up.

Don’t do what they ask on the computer. To be sure, call the company back yourself, on a number you already have, such as the one on your bank card.

AurOS will never ask you to let someone connect to your computer, or for card numbers, passwords, bank details or gift cards. Anyone who does is not from AurOS, Microsoft or your bank. A page that says your computer is infected, and gives a number to call, is the same trick.

A program that lets someone in. If AurOS says “Before you install this”, the program lets someone far away use your computer. Press Don’t install it. Press I called someone I know only if YOU called a person you know, and they asked you to.

If you already let someone in

It happens to careful people every day. It isn’t your fault. Act now:

  1. Press Turn off, then Turn it off. That cuts them off.
  2. Call your bank on the number on the back of your card. Tell them what happened.
  3. From another phone or computer, change the passwords for your email and your bank.
  4. Ask someone you trust to check this computer before you bank on it again.
