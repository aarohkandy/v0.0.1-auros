Video calls

Most video calls work in Internet. Someone sends you a link, you press it, and you are in the call.

Join a call from a link

  1. Open the email or message with the link, and press the link. Internet opens the call.
  2. If the page offers to download a program, look for “Join from your browser” and press that instead.
  3. Internet asks whether the website may use your camera and microphone. If you were expecting the call, press Allow.
  4. Type your name if it asks, then press the button to join.

This works for Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams and many others.

If something doesn’t work

  • If they can’t see or hear you: on the call page, press a crossed-out microphone or camera to switch it back on.
  • If you pressed Block by mistake: press the small camera picture at the left end of the address bar, remove the block, then press the round arrow to load the page again.
  • Check that the camera isn’t covered. Some laptops have a little slider over it.
  • If you can’t hear them, see Bigger words, louder sound.

Only allow the camera and microphone for a call you were expecting. At any other time, press Block.
