My pictures and my Windows files

Your files from Windows are still in Windows. Nothing was deleted.

Look at your pictures

If you can’t see Photos, press All my programs first. Then press Photos. It shows your pictures. Or press Your files, and open the folder called Pictures.

Your files from Windows

When AurOS first asked “Does everything work?” and you said yes, it offered to copy your files. If you pressed Bring my files across, they are in Your files now, in the folders Documents, Pictures, Music and Videos.

A file that Windows keeps only on the internet, in OneDrive, is not copied.

If you pressed Not now, your files are still in Windows. To bring them across later, press Settings at the bottom of the screen, then Bring my files across. Or use Windows once to look at them: Going back to Windows shows how.

A USB stick

A USB stick that you plug in shows up in Your files.
