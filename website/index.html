Questions.

Will I lose my files?

AurOS is made to keep your files, but any change to a drive carries some risk. Copy the photos you treasure onto a memory stick first.

Will my Windows programs work?

No. AurOS has its own programs for the internet, photos, music and notes, and many more, free.

Does it cost anything later?

No. No ads and no subscription.

What if I get stuck?

Print the one-page guide for whoever helps you. AurOS will never phone you or ask for a card number, a password or gift cards.
