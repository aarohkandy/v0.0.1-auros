Is this safe? Who made it? What does it cost?

Is this safe?

AurOS is made to keep Windows and your files, and to let you go back. Any change to a drive carries some risk, so copy the photos you treasure onto a memory stick first. Edge and Windows will warn you about the file, because few people have downloaded it yet and it has no company name on it. Step 1 shows each warning and what to press.

Who made it?

One person, with the help of AI software. Not Microsoft. Anyone can read the source code. Write to aarohkandy@gmail.com.

Make it feel like home.

Three steps. About 25 to 60 minutes.

AurOS needs at least 28 GB of empty room on your Windows drive. Setup checks before it changes anything.

  1. In Edge: Downloads

    AurOS-Setup.exe isn’t commonly downloaded. …
    Delete Keep

    Then Edge asks again:

    CancelDelete
    Keep anyway

    1Step 1: Download it

    Move the mouse over the file. Press the three dots, then Keep. Edge asks again: press the small arrow beside Delete, then Keep anyway.

    Do not press Delete. If you did, just press Get AurOS again.

    Using Chrome or Firefox? Its words are here.

  2. Windows protected your PC

    App: AurOS-Setup.exe

    Run anywayDon’t run

    2Step 2: Say yes to Windows

    Press Open file. If Windows shows a blue box, press More info, then Run anyway. Then press Yes, the white button.

Every screen, with pictures

If your antivirus takes the file, or Windows says Smart App Control blocked it, nothing has been changed. What to do

If a page that says 404 or Not found opens instead, the download isn’t ready. Nothing is wrong with your computer. Press the back arrow at the top left of your browser and try again in an hour. Is AurOS working today?
