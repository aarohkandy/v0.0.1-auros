Download the Windows file anyway

It asks what you’d like to do. Then it gets out of the way.

Words, not puzzles.

Every button says what it does: Internet, Photos, Your files. No little pictures to guess at.

The buttons you need never move.

Help, Wi-Fi, Bigger text and Turn off live along the bottom, always. This page has the same bar. Go on, press Bigger text.

Four looks. Pick the one that feels like home.

You pick one while AurOS goes on. To change it later, press Settings, then How this computer works.

Putting it on takes three steps.

Your browser and Windows haven’t met AurOS before, so they ask if you trust it. They ask about every new program. Press the button in the numbered circle.

  1. Downloads

    AurOS-Setup.exe

    Keep

    1Step 1: Download it

    Press Get AurOS. Your browser asks if you trust the file. Press Keep, then Keep anyway.

  2. Windows protected your PC

    App: AurOS-Setup.exe

    Run anyway

    2Step 2: Say yes to Windows

    3Step 3: Pick a look, then install

See every screen, with pictures

Changed your mind? Go back.

After the restart, AurOS asks if everything works. Until you say yes, Windows and your files are meant to stay on this computer.

Press No, go back to Windows and Windows starts the next time. Nothing is deleted.

The honest bit.

Before you press anything, here’s what isn’t done yet.

  • It hasn’t been on a real computer yet.

    So far it has been tried on a virtual Windows 11 computer, not a real one. You’d be one of the first.

  • Your Windows programs won’t come with you.

    AurOS has its own programs for the internet, photos, music and notes, and many more, free.

  • Changing a drive is never risk-free.

    AurOS is made to keep Windows and your files. Still, copy the photos you treasure onto a memory stick first.

  • Wishes isn’t switched on yet.

    It’s the part we’re proudest of. Here it is.

Questions people ask us.

Is it free?

Yes. No ads and no subscription.

Will I lose my files?

AurOS is made to keep your files, but any change to a drive carries some risk. Copy the photos you treasure onto a memory stick first.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. If you can press a button that says what it does, you can use AurOS. And Help is always at the bottom of the screen.

What if I get stuck?

Press Help at the bottom of this page. It opens a one-page guide to print for whoever helps you. AurOS will never phone you or ask for a card number, a password or gift cards.
